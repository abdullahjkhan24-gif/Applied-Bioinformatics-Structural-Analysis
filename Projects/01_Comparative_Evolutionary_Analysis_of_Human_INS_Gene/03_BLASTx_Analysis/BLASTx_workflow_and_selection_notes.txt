BLASTx WORKFLOW AND HOMOLOG SELECTION NOTES

Instructor workflow:
NCBI nucleotide sequence -> BLASTx -> identify homologous protein sequences -> select approximately 25–30 homologs using high query coverage, high percentage identity and low E-value -> download selected homologs as FASTA -> include the query sequence in the sequence dump before MEGA alignment.
